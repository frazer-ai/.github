# Security policy

This policy covers every repository in the `frazer-ai` organization that does
not have its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@frazer.ai** with:

- what is affected (product, component, version or URL);
- how to reproduce it;
- the impact you expect.

Please do not open a public issue or pull request for a vulnerability, and do
not access data that is not yours. We acknowledge reports within 2 business
days and keep you informed until the issue is fixed.
